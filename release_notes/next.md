# DesktopWidget 1.1.1

## Auto arrange
- New in Settings: Auto arrange tidies your desktop with one click
- Pick a style and see it on your real desktop right away, then keep it or cancel:
  - Tidy up: your own layout, lined up and evenly spaced
  - Balanced: widgets spread over both sides, with the middle free
  - Dashboard: one neat block in the middle of the screen
  - Bottom dock: centred rows along the bottom
- Your widgets keep their sizes, and Undo puts everything back the way it was
- Also available from the layouts button on the + bar and by typing "arrange" in the quick launcher

## Improvements
- Saved layouts now also remember how wide each folder is
