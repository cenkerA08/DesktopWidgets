# DesktopWidget 1.1.0

## A whole new look
- Everything is redesigned in the Windows 11 style: calm surfaces, crisp icons and one accent colour
- Settings is laid out like Windows Settings, with on/off switches and grouped cards
- 11 new themes in dark and light; your current theme switches to the closest new one
- New "Match wallpaper" theme: colours taken from your wallpaper, and it updates when your wallpaper changes

## Folder stacks
- Put several folders in one widget and flip between them with tabs (or scroll over the tabs)
- Create one from Add widget, or right-click a folder and choose "Stack with other folders"

## Quick launcher
- Press Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere, type a few letters and press Enter to open any app, game, file or layout
- Finds things by initials too ("rdr" finds Red Dead Redemption); apps you use often come first
- Change or turn off the shortcut in Settings

## Layouts
- Save how your desktop is arranged (positions, which widgets are on, the theme) and switch with one click
- Switch from the new layouts button on the + bar, from Settings, or from the quick launcher

## Organize folders
- Suggests a better folder for apps that are in the wrong place, using Steam's genres for your games
- Finds the same app in two folders, shortcuts that no longer work, uninstalled Steam games and empty folders
- You review everything before it changes, and you can undo it afterwards

## Changes
- The focus view is gone; open apps straight from your widgets or the quick launcher
- The + bar now also has search and layouts buttons

## Bug fixes
- Widgets no longer pile up in the top-left corner after signing in to Windows; they go back to where you left them
- Unplugging a monitor no longer loses the positions of the widgets that were on it
