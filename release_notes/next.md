# DesktopWidget 1.0.53

## New look
- All widgets now share one design: soft colour glow, rounded panels and matching labels
- System stats redesigned with a big live graph and coloured cards
- The media player gets a separate panel for progress and controls
- Settings, Add widget and the app picker match the new style
- New focus view: the selected app is shown large above a shelf of your apps (arrow keys move, Enter opens, up/down switches folders)

## Faster
- Starts more than twice as fast, and Settings and the focus view open much quicker

## Bug fixes
- Games added from desktop shortcuts keep working (and show their icon) after the shortcut is deleted
- Icon and tile size settings are kept after a restart
- If your settings file is ever damaged, a backup copy is kept instead of losing your folders
