# DesktopWidget 1.0.52

## Looks
- App icons are sharper: games and Store apps now use their high-resolution icons
- App tiles have a cleaner design with a clear hover effect, in folders and in the focus view
- Longer app names fit on their tiles
- Widget corners no longer show dark pixels on your wallpaper
- The close button highlights when you hover over it

## Bug fixes
- Fixed a crash when moving the mouse over the buttons in the focus view
- Icon size and tile size settings are now kept after restarting
